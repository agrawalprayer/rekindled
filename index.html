What I work on

Three connected threads
01 · Self-assembly

Programmable matter

How three-dimensional structures emerge from simple local interaction rules, and how symmetry, designability and assembly complexity shape the reachable phenotype space.

02 · Mathematics

Graphs & combinatorics

Counting and characterising connectivity-preserving growth processes, including successive vertex orderings of connected graphs.

03 · Information

Algorithmic complexity

Understanding how information and minimal descriptions constrain which structures can be assembled efficiently.
